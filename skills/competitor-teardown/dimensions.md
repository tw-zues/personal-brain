# Competitor Teardown — Dimension Rubrics

9 dimensions, scored 0-3 each. **Sales Motion (#5) gets a 6-sub-dimension deep dive.** **Revenue Mix Inference (#9) answers "what do they actually sell the most."**

## Scoring scale (universal)
- **0** — Absent / unclear / actively bad
- **1** — Present but weak / generic / unconvincing
- **2** — Solid execution, recognizably good
- **3** — Distinctive / best-in-class / something to actively learn from

Every score needs a quoted evidence snippet and a confidence tag.

---

## 1. Positioning clarity

Can a buyer tell what they do, who it's for, and why them within 10 seconds?

- 0 — Generic ("We build software that scales")
- 1 — One of {what / who / why} is clear
- 2 — All three present but pedestrian
- 3 — Sharp, specific, memorable

## 2. ICP / vertical focus

Generalist or specialist? Look for industry-specific case studies, landing pages, copy.

- 0 — Pure generalist
- 1 — Generalist with industries listed
- 2 — Clear primary verticals (2-4) with dedicated content
- 3 — Single-vertical specialist with full content stack

## 3. Case study quality

Count, recency, named customers, hard numbers, vertical density, format.

- 0 — No case studies or all generic blurbs
- 1 — A few, mostly anonymous or numberless
- 2 — Solid bank, named customers, some hard numbers
- 3 — Many recent, named, numbered case studies in tight verticals

## 4. Social proof

Logo walls, testimonials, awards, certifications, years in business, team size.

## 5. SALES MOTION — DEEP DIVE

### 5A. Front-door friction
Primary CTA type, placement, form fields, chat, phone, friction level.

### 5B. Lead magnets / value-first offers
Free audits, calculators, downloadables, reports, webinars, newsletters, tools.

### 5C. Discovery / qualification process
"How we work" page, discovery framing, time commitment, buyer-education content.

### 5D. Productization & pricing transparency
Pricing visibility, project minimum, packages, sprints, retainers, tiers.

### 5E. Risk-reversal & trust accelerators
Guarantees, SLAs, free deliverables, IP ownership, team stability, methodology.

### 5F. Sales-specific content & nurture channels
Comparison pages, ROI calculators, vertical landing pages, LinkedIn, podcast, newsletter.

### Sales motion verdict
Identify the archetype: Talk-to-sales gate / Free audit funnel / Productized fixed-fee / Education-first nurture / Self-serve book-now / Free tool/community pull / Hybrid.

## 6. Differentiator claims

Stated differentiators, methodology, talent claims, speed claims, IP-ownership language.

## 7. Content marketing

Blog cadence, topic mix, long vs short form, SEO signals, lead magnets, original research.

## 8. Trust + team signals

Team page, headcount, locations, founded year, credentials, hiring activity.

## 9. Revenue mix inference

What they actually sell the most vs what they market. Pull from: Clutch profile, hiring signals, lead magnet topics, service page depth, case study distribution, headcount geography.
